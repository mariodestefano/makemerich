Make Me Rich

Probably the most honest app on Google Play.

It does exactly one thing.
Open the app and discover your position in a real global visitor counter shared by everyone who has ever opened it.

Are you visitor #47? Visitor #100,000?
There's only one way to find out.

Download on Google Play

Features

  • 🔢 A real-time global visitor counter
  • 💸 Ads, because the name demands it
  • 🎬 An optional "Watch an Ad" button for true enthusiasts
  • 🌍 Works worldwide in English
  • ⚡ No account, no settings, no distractions

Why Does This Exist?

Sometimes an app doesn't need to solve a problem. Sometimes it just needs a great name.


Make Me Rich won't pay you, manage your finances, or make you wealthy. It will simply show you a number and a few ads. That's the deal.
